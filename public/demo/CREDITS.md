# Asset Credits & Provenance

All image assets in this project are custom-generated for the Kaaravan marketplace demo:

1. **Demo Catalog Images (`/demo/*.webp`)**:
   - 40 Product images (`prod-1.webp` through `prod-40.webp`)
   - 10 Category images (`cat-*.webp`)
   - 3 Hero banners (`banner-1.webp`, `banner-2.webp`, `banner-3.webp`)
   - Generated using Google Gemini Flash Image (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image). All catalog images are WebP formatted, max 1600px and under 250 KB; hero banners are 1920×800 (cover crop) and under 350 KB with typography negative space.

2. **Kaaravan Brand Identity Assets (`/brand/*`, `/app/icon.png`)**:
   - Realistic circular brand logo emblem and symbols featuring the camel caravan journey, celestial guiding star, and Pakistani geometric filigree in deep teal, warm gold, and terracotta.
   - Generated on 2026-10-04 using Gemini Flash Image tool, masked and optimized into WebP, PNG, and favicon formats.
   - Note: Temporary AI concept asset per docs/ASSET_RECORD.md.

3. **Seller Logos (`/demo/seller-*.svg`)**:
   - Custom SVG emblems created for the 3 registered demo sellers (Multan Kashikari, Peshawar Heritage Leather, Hunza Organic Reserve).

All unused SVG placeholder files have been removed. No third-party, unlicensed, or copyrighted images are used in this project.
